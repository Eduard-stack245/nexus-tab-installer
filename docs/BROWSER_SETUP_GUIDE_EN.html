NEXUS TAB INSTALLER · CONNECTING BROWSERS

Connect your browser once

Once connected, the program gets links to Nexus Mods mods from all open windows and tabs of the chosen profile, even tabs in the background. No need to switch tabs by hand.

1. Choose your browser

2. Confirm the connection

Nexus Tab Installer → My mods → Connect a browser
Allow the browser to pass open Nexus Mods tabs?

Other sites and the browser history are not sent to the program.

✓ Allow  or Decline

Then click the Nexus Tab Collector icon in the browser toolbar and choose "Check the connection and refresh the list".

3. Downloading mods

Go to "My mods", press "Find mods", tick the files and press "Download selected". Files go one by one with pauses: the pace is chosen in the "Settings". Keep the browser open. Nexus may ask for a login or a check: the browser window comes to the front and the download goes on after your action.

The rules of Nexus forbid bots and downloading far beyond normal use, and an account can be suspended. A slow pace lowers the load and the risk but does not remove it.

If the popup of the extension shows "Allow access to Nexus", press that button: without access to the site files will not download. The extensions are not published in stores and are loaded from a folder.

The program and the extension work locally on your computer. With several browser profiles, the extension is installed into each profile separately.
